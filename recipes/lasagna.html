How to make Beef Lasagna

Description

This hearty beef lasagna layers rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and tender pasta sheets for a comforting family favorite. Ground beef is browned and simmered with tomatoes, garlic, and Italian herbs until deep and flavorful, then layered between sheets of lasagna noodles, a smooth white sauce, and generous handfuls of mozzarella and parmesan. Baked until golden and bubbling, it's a satisfying dish perfect for weeknight dinners or feeding a crowd, and it reheats beautifully the next day.

Ingredients

  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 onion finely chopped
  • 500g gr
  • 500g ground beef
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • cans (400g each) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • salt and pepper, to taste
  • 9-12 lasagna sheets (fresh or dried)
  • 50g (3 tbsp) butter
  • 50g (1/3 cup) plain flour
  • 500ml (2 cups) milk
    • 500ml (2 cups) milk
  • 200g (2 cups) shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Heat a splash of oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
  2. Add the ground beef and cook, breaking it up, until browned all over.
  3. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, and basil. Season with salt and pepper. Simmer uncovered for 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.
  4. While the sauce simmers, make the béchamel: melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat, then whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually whisk in the milk, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens and is smooth. Season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg.
  5. Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F)
  6. In a baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom. Add a layer of lasagna sheets, then more meat sauce, then a layer of béchamel. Repeat the layers, finishing with béchamel on top.
  7. Sprinkle the mozzarella and parmesan evenly over the top layer.
  8. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15-20 minutes, until golden and bubbling.
  9. Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
